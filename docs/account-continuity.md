# Account continuity and recovery

Last reviewed: October 27, 2026.

## Build habits that protect your work

Use an email account you control and secure its recovery methods. Use available authentication protections. Keep your device updated, restrict untrusted browser extensions, and avoid sharing signed-in sessions. Never provide a reseller with a password, one-time code, OAuth token, session cookie, or remote access to your Mac.

Record billing ownership privately and export important work when appropriate. Keep source code in version control and project knowledge in portable files. Avoid making your only copy of an important deliverable dependent on a service login.

Treat changes in travel, billing, organization membership, and network setup as occasions to recheck the current requirements. There is no documented “warm-up” chat schedule, minimum account age, or daily activity pattern that this project can verify as protection against suspension. Anecdotes should remain labeled as anecdotes.

## If access changes

First distinguish a service incident, failed renewal, wrong login, usage limit, and account restriction. Each requires a different remedy. The troubleshooting table gives a starting point. Do not assume that every error is a ban or that every ban is an IP issue.

For a restriction, preserve the message and date privately and follow [Anthropic's safeguards and appeals process](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8241253-safeguards-warnings-and-appeals). The official page lists several possible reasons, including unsupported-location account creation and policy violations. The account's own notice and support response are the relevant evidence; outside observers cannot reliably diagnose its enforcement reason.

## Prepare a factual appeal

Submit through the official logged-in appeal flow. Use a short factual description:

```text
I am requesting a review of my account restriction.
Observed message: [exact text, excluding secrets]
First observed: [date and timezone]
Relevant context: [accurate account and access facts]
Troubleshooting already attempted: [brief list]
Please clarify the reason and whether any corrective action is available.
```

Provide identifying or verification material only through a verified private support channel when requested. This template does not promise reinstatement. The official appeals page also describes available data-export and deletion options for restricted accounts.

## Protect your billing separately

If access is lost, verify whether renewal remains active through the actual purchase platform. Contact [official support](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/9015913-how-to-get-support) if you cannot reach billing settings. Recovery and billing are separate questions; do not assume either reinstatement or an automatic refund.
